IRYNA WALLPAPER & ACCENT WALLS — LOCAL CONCEPT

Launch on macOS: double-click preview.command, then visit http://127.0.0.1:4187
Or in Terminal from this folder:
python3 -m http.server 4187 --bind 127.0.0.1
Keep the terminal window open; Control-C stops the server.
If port 4187 is occupied, use 4188 instead and visit http://127.0.0.1:4188.
You can also open index.html directly; there are no build dependencies.

This concept remains local and unpublished. A public GitHub repository and an empty Netlify project were added
at the user’s request. The website remains unpublished. No forms, analytics,
remote fonts, or external image requests. Phone and email links open the
visitor's own calling/email application. No contact was initiated during QA.
All visual assets are original SVG illustrations, labeled on the page.
netlify.toml deliberately blocks a standard Netlify build. noindex and
robots.txt discourage indexing; local loopback binding keeps the preview
on this computer. These are not a substitute for hosted access control.
